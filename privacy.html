Privacy Policy

Construction Calculator · Last updated: 25 September 2026

This app is built to work entirely on your device. We do not run any server, we do not collect analytics, and we do not show ads.

Data we collect

None. The app has no user accounts and does not gather, transmit, or sell any personal data. Every calculation you make and every project you save is stored only in the app's local storage on your device. If you uninstall the app, its local data is removed. Device backups may retain a copy according to your system settings.

Permissions

The app requests no runtime permissions — no camera, no location, no contacts, no storage access. It includes the standard Apple App Store and Google Play billing capability, which is used solely to sell and restore the optional one-time "Pro" unlock.

Network use

The app does not connect to any server of ours. The only network activity is handled by Apple App Store or Google Play, to process and restore the "Pro" in-app purchase. No calculation data, project, or personal information is sent anywhere as part of that.

Sharing

If you choose to share a calculation or export it as a PDF, that content leaves your device only through the app or recipient you pick (for example a messaging app). This happens only when you explicitly tap Share or Export — the app never shares anything on its own.

Children

The app is a professional tool and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

In-app purchase

The optional "Pro" unlock is a one-time purchase processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. The store's own privacy terms apply to that transaction. We never receive your payment details. Purchase identifiers and Pro access are stored locally.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
