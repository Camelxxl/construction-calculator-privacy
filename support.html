Construction Calculator — Support

Feet-inch fractions and jobsite math for iPhone and Android

Construction Calculator handles feet-inch-fraction math, area, volume, right triangles, concrete, tile, paint, roof pitch, stairs, board feet and unit conversion, and shows how it got every result. It works offline, with no account and no ads.

Contact

Found a bug, a wrong result, or missing a calculation? Email marouane.nagh@googlemail.com. Please include your device, OS version and, for a calculation, the values you entered. We usually reply within two business days.

Restoring Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID or Google account. On a new device or after reinstalling, open Settings → Restore Purchases while signed in to the same account.
